PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------

Renewable energy projects face revenue volatility that blocks financing. A solar farm may produce 1000 MWh one month, then 700 MWh during monsoons. Banks won't lend against unpredictable cash flows.

SolarPunk Protocol creates a revenue floor using financial derivatives. Solar operators buy insurance (put options) that pay out when production drops below target. This stabilizes cash flow, enabling project finance.

---

SOLUTION / HOW IT WORKS
-----------------------

Three components:
1. Smart contracts (Solidity) - holds collateral, enforces settlement
2. Pricing engine (Python) - calculates premiums using NASA solar data
3. Dashboard (React) - user interface for hedging
