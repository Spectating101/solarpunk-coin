## Source hierarchy

The current integrated thesis drafts are **research inventories**, not authorities. Smooth prose does not validate a coefficient, model output, institutional claim, or implementation result.

Authority runs in this direction:

```text
underlying data / official public documents / code / proof artifacts
        ↓
source inventory and result ledger
        ↓
claim registry
        ↓
comparative institutional mapping
        ↓
frozen thesis argument and replication package
        ↓
Policy Lab research publication and later extensions
```

Comparative institutional evidence must preserve its own claim class:

```text
observed rule or process
≠ institutional inference
≠ CL–ECI analogy
≠ simulation proposal
≠ external validation
```

Historical SolarPunk/SPK material remains genuine project history and implementation evidence, but it must not silently control the final thesis ontology or current Policy Lab semantics.

## One-paragraph state

The research programme now has a consolidated foundation connecting three distinct layers. **ECI** is the empirical admissibility layer: it evaluates whether an energy-linked signal deserves operational weight. **Constrained Ledger** is the institutional constraint layer: it specifies reliable evidence, rule-bound issuance and anti-reuse, explicit uncertainty, defined settlement, and limited governance. **Policy Lab** is the executable research layer: it represents those distinctions through versioned cases, policies, typed constraint evaluations, binding quantity ceilings, settlement outcomes, and reproducible receipts. The Norway institutional-evidence package adds a heavy external reference environment: Elhub, Guarantees of Origin / NECS, and flexibility-market admission show that evidence quality, authority, quantity, registry identity, anti-reuse, settlement, and correction are real operational problems in mature energy institutions. Norway does not validate CL–ECI or an energy-backed currency. The immediate priority remains to audit and freeze the research inventory, result ledger, claim registry, pricing/model-risk analysis, instrument review, and thesis contribution architecture. Reference simulations should follow approved claim and source registers rather than lead the research.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Do not:

- treat any old integrated thesis draft as the final analysis;
- retain a reported number without a reproducible source and script path;
- let a dashboard, token, smart contract, or signature upgrade weak evidence;
- treat modeled resource context as observed production;
- treat attribution as settlement or a receipt as delivery;
- treat public-network execution as proof of reserves, law, market value, or production security;
- treat controlled cases as independent validation;
- treat institutional analogy as external validation;
- write that Norway validates CL–ECI, Policy Lab, SPK, or energy-backed money;
- treat open aggregate Elhub data as attributable site-level issuance evidence;
- claim a completed currency, stablecoin, monetary system, or market adoption;
- rename the overall historical project to `Constraint` merely because that namespace remains in code.
