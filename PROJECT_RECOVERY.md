## One-paragraph state

The academic architecture is now frozen upstream of the software. **ECI** owns Boundary 1: whether an energy-linked signal deserves economic interpretation. **Constrained Ledger** supplies the claim-level evidence and binding-constraint conditions tested at Boundaries 2–3, while the synthesis paper fixes the final four-boundary sequence: energy adjacency → economic information → claim-level evidence → binding constraint → monetary performance, with legal/regulatory status separately assessed. **Policy Lab** is the executable research layer. Its existing `Evidence → Assurance → Policy/Admission → Quantity → Settlement → Receipt` pipeline remains useful, but it is an implementation decomposition rather than the academic boundary chain. The current controlled prototype demonstrates deterministic evidence gating, quantity limitation, settlement replay, lineage, and reproduction; it does not establish economically optimal rules, production-suitable pricing, bounded governance, legal enforceability, or Boundary-4 monetary performance. The immediate work is therefore semantic reconciliation, Conformance v1 cleanup/execution, External Case 001, and then a derived four-boundary assessment layer — not another broad research rewrite or product expansion.
