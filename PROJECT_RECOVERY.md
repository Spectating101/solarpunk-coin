## One-paragraph state

The research programme now has a consolidated foundation connecting three distinct layers. **ECI** is the empirical admissibility layer: it evaluates whether an energy-linked signal deserves operational weight. **Constrained Ledger** is the institutional constraint layer: it specifies reliable evidence, rule-bound issuance and anti-reuse, explicit uncertainty, defined settlement, and limited governance. **Policy Lab** is the executable research layer: it represents those distinctions through versioned cases, policies, typed constraint evaluations, binding quantity ceilings, settlement outcomes, and reproducible receipts. The immediate priority is to audit and freeze the research inventory, result ledger, claim registry, pricing/model-risk analysis, instrument review, and thesis contribution architecture. Public packaging should present the programme as an academic research portfolio with one flagship executable laboratory. The next interface-design pass should be developed through CLI wireframes and a reduced guided assessment before any broad frontend rewrite. The existing Policy Lab code and public workbench are important assets, but their thesis-facing release must eventually be frozen against verified research objects and exact source revisions.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Do not:

- treat any old integrated thesis draft as the final analysis;
- retain a reported number without a reproducible source and script path;
- let a dashboard, token, smart contract, or signature upgrade weak evidence;
- treat modeled resource context as observed production;
- treat attribution as settlement or a receipt as delivery;
- treat public-network execution as proof of reserves, law, market value, or production security;
- treat controlled cases as independent validation;
- claim a completed currency, stablecoin, monetary system, or market adoption;
- rename the overall historical project to `Constraint` merely because that namespace remains in code;
- treat a competition, homepage, or interface mode as a separate research ontology;
- begin a broad visual rewrite before the flagship states, invariants, final assessment, and component mapping are frozen.
