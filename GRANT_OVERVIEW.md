# GRANT OVERVIEW

## One-sentence pitch

SolarPunk is renewable-energy financial infrastructure that turns verified energy value into programmable settlement, hedging, and treasury flows.

## Plain-English explanation

Renewable projects face volatile prices and weak hedging access. SolarPunk provides a protocol layer that can:

- represent energy value in contract logic
- enforce margin and liquidation rules
- route fees into reserve/insurance/ops/audit budgets
