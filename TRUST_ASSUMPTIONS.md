# TRUST ASSUMPTIONS

## Purpose

Make explicit what must be trusted today for the protocol to behave as intended.

## Current assumptions

1. **Role operators act honestly**  
   Admin/oracle/slasher roles are trusted not to abuse privileged paths.

2. **Oracle/data quality is sufficient**  
   Inputs used for pricing and settlement are assumed timely and accurate enough for current stage.

3. **Off-chain ops follow policy**  
   Deployment evidence, receipt publication, and operational checks are performed consistently.

4. **Pilot phase is controlled**  
   Early usage is expected to be constrained while controls are hardened.

5. **No claim of full decentralization yet**  
   Governance and control surfaces are still evolving and should be described honestly.

## Assumptions to reduce next

- migrate critical controls toward stronger governance patterns
- harden oracle/data verification and monitoring
- increase independent review via external audit
