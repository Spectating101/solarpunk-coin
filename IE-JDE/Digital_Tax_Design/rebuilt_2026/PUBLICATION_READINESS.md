5. Stage-A legal architecture versus Stage-B procedural-capacity separation;

## Mandatory pre-submission gates

### 1. Independent path/source spot recode

An independent reviewer should re-code a sample covering at least:

- Thailand continuous-process boundary;
- Philippine direct-to-NRDSP payment boundary;
- Vietnam order/payment functionality;
- Indonesia appointed-collector activation;
- one Stage-B procedural row.

The goal is not to obtain a second opinion on policy desirability; it is to test whether the coding can be reproduced from the cited primary sources.

### 2. Final legal-currentness sweep

Immediately before submission, recheck all operative laws/guidance used in the manuscript, especially:

- Vietnam 2025/2026 decree stack;
- Philippine 2025 digital-service implementation material;
- current Malaysian MySToDS/MySST guidance;
- Thai VES/code guidance;
- Indonesian PMSE VAT procedures and reported administrative facts.

Any material update must flow through source catalog -> claim/path matrix -> manuscript -> CI.

### 3. Venue selection / format

Choose a venue that accepts comparative tax-administration/institutional work rather than requiring a causal policy-treatment estimate as the primary contribution.

Verify current:

- article type and word limit;
- abstract requirements;
- reference/footnote style;
- legal citation expectations;
- table/figure and online-appendix limits;
- preprint/related-work rules;
- anonymization requirements.

### 4. Visible legal-citation pass

`MANUSCRIPT_SOURCE_MAP.csv` is the internal authority, but the final manuscript needs venue-standard visible citations/footnotes for statutes, regulations, decrees, guidance, and administrative figures.

### 5. Related-work overlap check

Run a final manuscript text/table comparison against Invisible Ledger and disclose related public work where venue policy requires it.

## Optional improvements, not blockers

- identify one additional analytically useful negative/external case;
- gather explanatory evidence on why specific nodes were selected;
- develop a later within-country causal design if harmonized outcomes become available;
- add more comparable governance/compliance-cost evidence.

Do not delay this paper solely to grow the catalogue.

## Stop rule

Do not reopen the old cross-country rate/DiD lane or invent a country maturity score merely to make the paper look more quantitative.

A conceptual redesign is justified only if:

- primary legal sources contradict the path coding;
- independent recoding cannot reproduce the core classifications;
- prior-art review finds the transaction-path/countercase/Stage-A-B contribution already fully replicated elsewhere;
- peer review identifies a substantive design flaw.

Otherwise, further work should be venue formatting, external checking, and peer-review response—not internal expansion.
