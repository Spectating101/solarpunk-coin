# Fiscal Choke Points — Hostile Reviewer Risk Register

This file records the strongest foreseeable objections to the rebuilt paper and the minimum response required before final submission.

## R1 — “Fiscal choke point is just new jargon for platform VAT collection.”

**Severity:** critical  
**Current response:** strong internally; external novelty test still required

### Why the objection is credible

OECD and tax-administration literature already discuss full platform VAT/GST liability, vendor collection, third-party information, and firms as fiscal intermediaries.

### Required defense

The paper must state early that the mechanism is prior art and defend a narrower contribution:

- common transaction-node architecture across unlike instruments;
- five components;
- node locus;
- event coupling;
- application across five cases without pooling unlike revenue measures.

### Closure evidence

- `LITERATURE_POSITIONING.md` complete;
- literature section in manuscript complete;
- no sentence claims invention of platform tax intermediation;
- source catalog includes the direct prior-art literature.

**Status:** `INTERNALLY CLOSED / EXTERNAL RETEST REQUIRED`

## R7 — “Your legal descriptions rely on outdated or weak guidance.”

**Severity:** high  
**Current response:** substantially closed for the central manuscript claims

### Country status

- **Malaysia:** current MySToDS portal supplies destination, threshold, and period rules; the current official legislation/guides index still links the FSP guide used for platform-control wording. Later rate/scope changes must come from current amendments rather than the 2021 guide.
- **Indonesia:** the current DGT digital-tax page controls current monthly remittance and periodic-return procedure; the older source remains historical context only.
- **Vietnam:** Decree 117 supplies the original July 2025 transaction trigger. Decree 68/2026 expressly carries the per-transaction platform withholding responsibility forward into the broader 2026 household/individual tax-administration framework. Decree 141/2026 subsequently amends specified Decree 68 thresholds; the package does not rely on those thresholds for the event-coupling claim.
- **Thailand:** the continuous-process platform rule is now supported by both Revenue Code section 82/13 and Revenue Department guidance.
- **Philippines:** 2025 BIR regulation and administrative issuances provide the current B2C/B2B paths used in the paper.

Comparable audit/reconciliation powers remain thinner across all five cases, but the current manuscript does not use that missing evidence to rank performance.

**Status:** `CENTRAL LEGAL CLAIMS CLOSED THROUGH 2026-09-14 / FINAL JUST-BEFORE-SUBMISSION CHECK REQUIRED`

## R11 — “Why these five components?”

**Severity:** medium-high  
**Current response:** strong

### Required defense

Each component corresponds to a necessary administrative question:

- object — what is taxed?;
- node — who acts?;
- nexus — why this jurisdiction?;
- rail — how is duty performed?;
- reconciliation — how can declaration be checked?

The framework is an analytical decomposition, not a claim that every statute uses these labels. Literature positioning ties the decomposition to established work on tax administration, information trails, and fiscal intermediation.

**Status:** `CLOSED FOR DRAFT`

## R13 — “ASEAN digital-tax comparison already exists, so what is new here?”

**Severity:** critical  
**Current response:** narrowed and internally strong

### Why the objection is credible

Government and academic work has already compared ASEAN digital-tax regimes, including VAT/VAT-like structures, rates, collection methods, nexus, and legal treatment.

### Required defense

Concede regional comparison as prior art. The paper must not claim to be the first ASEAN comparison. Its contribution is the **transaction-node architecture**:

- which actor is legally activated;
- which control function supports that assignment;
- through which operational rail the duty is performed;
- how tightly the fiscal action is coupled to the underlying commercial event.

### Closure evidence

- `SOURCE_CATALOG.csv` includes prior ASEAN-comparison controls `LIT-008` and `LIT-009`;
- `CLAIM_REGISTER.csv` includes `DT-LIT-002`;
- `LITERATURE_POSITIONING.md` explicitly concedes regional comparative prior art;
- final manuscript should not imply first-in-region status.

**Status:** `INTERNALLY CLOSED / EXTERNAL NOVELTY RETEST REQUIRED`
