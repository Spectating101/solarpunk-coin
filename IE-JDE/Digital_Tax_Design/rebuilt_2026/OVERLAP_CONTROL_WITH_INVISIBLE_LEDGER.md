# Fiscal Choke Points — Publication Overlap Control with Invisible Ledger

## Purpose

Fiscal Choke Points (DT) and Invisible Ledger (IL) share a broad interest in digital records, but they are separate papers. This control prevents the later consolidation idea from contaminating the current standalone contributions.

## DT owns the institutional tax-administration question

> How do governments convert commercial observability and transaction control into administrable fiscal responsibility?

DT's central evidence is the five-country/seven-path source-controlled legal and procedural comparison.

## IL owns the measurement/reconciliation question

> How does the interpretation of digital-commercial growth change as the observation layer moves across issuer accounts, national e-commerce statistics, payment traces, and administrative records?

IL's central evidence is the Indonesia cross-record empirical sequence.

## Permitted shared boundary

DT may rely on the general proposition that platform revenue, transaction value, seller turnover, GDP, taxable income, and unpaid tax are non-equivalent. That boundary is established measurement/accounting doctrine and does not make IL evidence part of DT.

DT must not use:

- IL transaction-revenue residuals;
- Tokopedia GTV/revenue divergence;
- BPS channel decomposition;
- Bank Indonesia payment growth;
- IL's old ASEAN wedge;

as evidence that a tax is owed or that a fiscal choke point is effective.

## PMK 37 rule

IL may discuss PMK 37 as the final stage in its visibility chain. DT's current central Indonesian path is PMSE VAT appointed-collector administration. Any future DT use of PMK 37 must be separately source-controlled and must not duplicate IL's narrative, table, or conclusion.

## No circular validation

The following are prohibited:

- “IL proves the activity exists, therefore DT should tax it”;
- “DT creates a reporting architecture, therefore IL's measurement is validated”;
- treating one paper as external validation of the other;
- reusing identical tables/figures as independent contributions without disclosure.

## Submission overlap check

Before submission of either paper:

1. compare manuscripts for distinctive sentence/paragraph overlap;
2. keep literature/background overlap minimal and independently phrased;
3. disclose related public working papers where venue rules require it;
4. ensure each abstract, introduction, results section, and conclusion can stand without the companion paper;
5. keep the future unified `record -> node -> use -> outcome` model explicitly outside both standalone contribution claims.

## Future consolidation

A later synthesis may connect measurement visibility to institutional action. That future synthesis is not a reason to broaden either current paper.
