# Fiscal Choke Points — AI Assistance Disclosure

## Purpose

This record exists so the submission package never requires a false declaration about manuscript preparation.

The research/manuscript workflow used OpenAI ChatGPT materially for research organization, source-audit planning, comparative-framework development, coding/validation support, drafting, rewriting, and language editing. The author directed the research questions and iterative decisions, reviewed source-backed claims and calculations, rejected earlier overclaims, and remains responsible for the final scholarly content.

AI is not an author and must not be listed as one.

## Suggested transparent declaration

Adapt this to the exact policy of the selected journal at submission time:

> **Declaration of AI-assisted manuscript preparation.** During preparation of this work, the author used OpenAI ChatGPT to assist with research organization, source-audit planning, manuscript structuring, drafting/revision, coding and validation support, and language editing. All substantive claims, source selections, calculations, legal interpretations, citations, and final wording were reviewed by the author, who takes full responsibility for the accuracy, originality, and integrity of the submitted manuscript. The AI system is not an author and was not used as a source of empirical or legal authority.

## If the journal asks for more granular disclosure

The author should accurately identify, as applicable:

- literature/search-query formulation and synthesis support;
- creation/refinement of the transaction-path architecture;
- drafting and revision of manuscript prose;
- Python/validation-code assistance;
- calculations subsequently validated against controlled data rows;
- source checking against official legal/administrative materials;
- copyediting and organization.

Do not claim that AI only performed grammar correction if its actual contribution was broader.

## Verification/accountability statement

The final submission should be treated as human-authored/accountable only after the author has personally reviewed:

- every primary legal source used for a material path classification;
- every headline administrative figure;
- every calculation generated by the controlled derivation/validation layer;
- the full manuscript and bibliography;
- the final AI disclosure itself.

## Route restrictions

Do not submit to a venue whose current policy requires a declaration that no AI was used in preparing, writing, editing or proofreading this manuscript. Under such a rule, the truthful response would make the manuscript ineligible in its current preparation history.

## Privacy/data note

The project uses public legal, administrative, institutional and academic sources. The publication package does not depend on confidential taxpayer or participant data being supplied to the AI system.
