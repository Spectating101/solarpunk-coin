# Invisible Ledger V2 — Support Package Manifest

**Status:** support infrastructure only; proposal/manuscript text remains externally controlled  
**Branch:** `invisible-ledger/full-capacity-2026`  
**Purpose:** build the empirical and reproducibility machinery underneath the September 2026 V2 research design without editing the active thesis proposal.

## Authority rule

The active September 2026 proposal/research-preview architecture governs the research question, scope, and inferential boundaries. This branch does **not** replace that proposal.

Legacy `Invisible_Economy` material on `main` contains residual-centric claims that no longer govern V2, including claims that transaction value minus platform revenue is hidden GDP, an unmeasured economy, or a tax gap. Those files remain historical evidence/source inventories unless independently re-admitted.

## Current V2 research sequence

1. **Issuer growth translation** — test whether transaction activity and recognized revenue provide the same longitudinal growth signal inside documented reporting perimeters.
2. **Mechanism reconciliation** — explain material divergences using monetization, incentives, principal/agent presentation, business mix, and reporting-perimeter changes where source evidence permits.
3. **Broader digital-growth anatomy** — use BPS business/channel evidence and Bank Indonesia traces to identify where growth appears outside issuer revenue.
4. **Institutional visibility** — distinguish record existence from legal reportability, transmission, matching, verified use, and observed outcomes.
5. **External corroboration** — retain ASEAN/global issuer evidence only as separate recurrence/heterogeneity evidence, not pooled causal proof.

## Frozen nonclaims for V2 support work

Do not promote any of the following without a separate evidentiary design:

- `transaction value - platform revenue = hidden economy`;
- platform-mediated transaction value = GDP, value added, participant income, taxable income, or unpaid tax;
- the old ASEAN-wide `$185B` calibration as a V2 result;
- the old `12.3x fiscal multiplier` as a V2 result;
- Malaysia LVG as causal validation of the old residual construct;
- calibrated ASEAN platform take rates as population parameters;
- payment-system values as one-for-one e-commerce sales;
- marketplace participation as causal formalization;
- legal reportability as proof of successful matching/compliance effects.

## Durable data products to build

1. `ISSUER_TRANSITION_LEDGER.csv` — one row per admitted issuer/segment transition.
2. `ISSUER_DEFINITION_LEDGER.csv` — source-native definitions, perimeter, geography, period, gross/net basis, inclusion/exclusion notes.
3. `TOKOPEDIA_RECONCILIATION.csv` — FY2022→FY2023 gross revenue, incentives, net revenue, GTV bridge and arithmetic decomposition.
4. `BPS_GROWTH_ANATOMY.csv` — national value, business count, implied value/business, marketplace/non-marketplace decomposition.
5. `BPS_BUSINESS_CHARACTERISTICS.csv` — recordkeeping/payment characteristics with sample/observational caveats.
6. `BI_DEFINITION_LEDGER.csv` — electronic-money, mobile-banking, QRIS and other traces with exact definitions and non-equivalence rules.
7. `INSTITUTIONAL_VISIBILITY_MATRIX.csv` — record exists → actor holds → legal duty → identifier → transmission → matching → verified use → outcome.
8. `SOURCE_REGISTER.csv` — exact publication/filing locators and source roles.
9. `EXCLUSION_LEDGER.csv` — rejected issuer-periods/metrics and reasons.
10. `RESULT_REPRODUCTION_MAP.csv` — final manuscript result → input rows → transformation → output artifact.

## Build principle

Results must be written from frozen tables and ledgers, not reconstructed from prose memory. A proposal sentence is not numerical authority when an official table or filing exists.

## Relationship to Digital Tax

Invisible Ledger remains a measurement/reconciliation project. Digital Tax/Fiscal Choke Points begins after a legally relevant taxable/reportable object is defined and studies which transaction node receives a bounded fiscal duty.

The two projects should eventually connect through a shared visibility chain, but neither may validate the other by circular inference.
