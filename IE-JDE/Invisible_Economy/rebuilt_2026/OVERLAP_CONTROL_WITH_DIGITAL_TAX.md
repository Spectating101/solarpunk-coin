# Invisible Ledger — Publication Overlap Control with Fiscal Choke Points

## Purpose

Invisible Ledger (IL) and Fiscal Choke Points (DT) are related but must remain independently publishable research objects. This file defines the permitted overlap before later portfolio consolidation.

## Central questions

### Invisible Ledger

> When digital-commercial activity grows, where does that growth become visible, why can different records imply different accounts of change, and what can be recovered by reconciling those records?

Primary domain: **measurement, accounting, statistics, payment traces, and institutional visibility**.

### Fiscal Choke Points

> How do governments convert commercial observability and transaction control into administrable fiscal responsibility?

Primary domain: **tax-administration architecture, liable nodes, transaction rails, legal activation, and operational capacity**.

## Permitted shared concepts

Both papers may state that:

- platform revenue, transaction value, seller turnover, payment flows, GDP, and taxable base are non-equivalent;
- digital intermediaries can generate transaction-linked records;
- legal reporting/withholding duties do not automatically prove successful matching or outcomes;
- PMK 37/2025 is a relevant Indonesian institutional development.

These are shared background/boundary facts, not shared central contributions.

## IL-exclusive empirical contributions

IL owns:

- Tokopedia FY2022-FY2023 GTV vs audited third-party net-revenue sign reversal;
- the audited gross-revenue / incentive / net-revenue reconciliation;
- BPS 2023-2024 national transaction-value and channel decomposition;
- BPS marketplace/non-marketplace business-characteristic comparisons;
- Bank Indonesia payment-trace comparison;
- the observation-layer sequence from issuer -> BPS -> BI -> administrative visibility;
- the decision-relevance rule for cross-record divergence.

DT should not reproduce these results as evidence for tax liability or tax performance.

## DT-exclusive contributions

DT owns:

- seven-path/five-country fiscal architecture;
- fiscal-choke-point construct;
- node locus / event coupling coding;
- Thailand/Philippines/Vietnam/Indonesia boundary cases;
- Stage-A legal architecture vs Stage-B procedural-capacity comparison;
- ASEAN cross-instrument source and claim registers;
- operational-capacity matrix.

IL should not reproduce these comparative ASEAN tax results.

## PMK 37 overlap rule

PMK 37 is the principal overlap risk.

### IL permitted use

IL uses PMK 37 only to answer the final observation-layer question:

`private record -> legal reportability -> operational activation -> matching/use/outcome`

The key IL result as of 2026-09-14 is the **gap between legal architecture and not-yet-observed operational activation/matching/outcomes**.

### DT permitted use

DT may use Indonesian tax administration within its ASEAN path comparison, but its core Indonesia path is the PMSE VAT appointed-collector architecture rather than reproducing IL's PMK 37 narrative as an empirical result.

### Prohibited duplication

- identical PMK paragraphs in both manuscripts;
- using IL's PMK module to validate the DT framework;
- using DT's ASEAN framework to claim IL generalizability;
- shared tables/figures presented as independent evidence in both papers without disclosure.

## Text-overlap policy

Before external submission:

1. compare both manuscripts for distinctive phrase overlap;
2. rewrite shared background independently where practical;
3. cite the companion working paper/preprint if venue norms require disclosure and both are public;
4. disclose common data/source material where materially overlapping;
5. never submit materially identical tables or prose as separate original contributions.

## Consolidation later

A later umbrella paper may connect:

`economic activity -> private/statistical record -> legal reportability -> fiscal node -> transmission -> matching -> verified use -> outcome`

That umbrella sequence is **not** a current empirical finding of either standalone paper. It is a future synthesis layer.
