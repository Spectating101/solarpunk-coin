## 1. Issuer evidence

### Tokopedia FY2022 → FY2023

Current V2 materials identify this as the cleanest mechanism case.

- transaction value growth: approximately **-8.90%**;
- third-party net segment revenue growth: approximately **+53.20%**;
- lower customer incentives account for approximately **60.56%** of the arithmetic increase in net revenue;
- higher gross revenue explains the remainder of the arithmetic net-revenue increase.

**Permitted interpretation:** transaction activity and recognized revenue can provide opposite growth signals within a documented business perimeter; accounting/monetization mechanisms can explain part of the divergence.

**Not permitted:** infer hidden GDP, seller income, taxable income, welfare, or platform-wide population parameters from the divergence.

## 2. BPS national growth anatomy

Current V2 materials report for 2023→2024:

- estimated e-commerce transaction value: **+17.08%**;
- estimated e-commerce business count: **+15.31%**;
- implied nominal value per estimated business: **+1.54%**.

**Interpretation:** aggregate value growth and participation growth carry different information; nominal expansion need not imply similarly large growth per estimated participating business.

**Freeze requirement:** rederive all three values from the exact official BPS 2023 and 2024 publication tables and store the input cells/units/publication vintage.

## 3. BPS channel decomposition

Current V2 materials report:

- marketplace component: **+1.45%**;
- non-marketplace component: **+20.57%**;
- approximately **98.46%** of the nominal published increase is attributed to the non-marketplace component in the current decomposition.

**Interpretation:** a marketplace-centered observation layer can miss where much of the published national increase occurs.

**Nonclaim:** this does not establish that marketplaces are economically unimportant, that channel choice causes growth, or that the categories are equivalent to issuer GMV/GTV.

**Freeze requirement:** retain exact BPS category definitions and verify whether category construction changed across publication vintages.

## 5. Bank Indonesia payment traces

Current V2 uses electronic-money, mobile-banking, QRIS, and related payment-system measures as supporting traces.

**Core rule:** each measure must retain its source-native definition. Payment value/volume is not one-for-one e-commerce sales, issuer GTV, output, income, or value added.

**Required ledger fields:**

- unit;
- frequency;

- revision status;
- allowed comparison;
- forbidden equivalence.

## 6. Institutional visibility

Current V2 sequence:

`RECORD EXISTS -> ACTOR HOLDS -> LEGAL DUTY -> IDENTIFIER -> TRANSMISSION -> MATCHING -> VERIFIED USE -> OUTCOME`

PMK 37/2025 and subsequent DGT material currently support a legal bridge between seller identity, transaction-linked turnover, withholding, and reporting.

**Current boundary:** legal reportability does not prove completed transmission, successful taxpayer matching, operational use, compliance improvement, or causal policy effect.

Every stage must therefore be coded separately as `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, or `UNKNOWN` with exact source IDs.

## 7. External corroboration

ASEAN/global issuer histories and the wider accounting panel may be used only to test recurrence/heterogeneity of measurement and accounting relationships.

**Rule:** no pooled ASEAN residual, no calibrated regional hidden-economy aggregate, and no cross-country causal inference from heterogeneous issuer definitions.
