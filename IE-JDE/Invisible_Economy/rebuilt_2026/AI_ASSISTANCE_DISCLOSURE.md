# Invisible Ledger — AI Assistance Disclosure

## Purpose

The current manuscript was materially developed with OpenAI ChatGPT assistance. The publication package must therefore preserve a truthful disclosure path and must never state that AI was not used.

AI assistance included research organization, source-audit planning, manuscript architecture, drafting/revision, calculation/validation support, source cross-checking, and language editing. The author directed the research questions and iterative decisions, reviewed evidence, rejected unsupported earlier claims, and remains fully responsible for the final submitted work.

AI is not an author and must not be listed as one.

## Suggested Elsevier-compatible declaration

Adapt to the exact current submission form/policy:

> **Declaration of AI and AI-assisted technologies in manuscript preparation.** During preparation of this work, the author used OpenAI ChatGPT to assist with research organization, source-audit planning, manuscript structuring, drafting and revision, calculation/validation support, source cross-checking, and language editing.
