## 2. Current research identity

The revised project does **not** estimate a hidden monetary stock or treat transaction value minus platform revenue as missing GDP, taxable income, or unpaid tax.

Its core question is:

> Where does digital-commercial growth become visible as activity moves across issuer accounts, business and channel statistics, payment systems, and administrative reporting architecture; when do those records imply different economic interpretations; and what can be learned by reconciling the difference?

The working definition of the **Invisible Ledger** is the fuller economic account recoverable from relationships, disagreements, and boundaries among partial records.

## 3. Empirical sequence

The V2 research sequence is deliberately ordered:

1. **Issuer translation** — compare transaction activity with recognized revenue within stable, documented reporting perimeters.
2. **Mechanism reconciliation** — explain material divergence where disclosures permit, including incentives, gross-versus-net presentation, monetization, business mix, and perimeter changes.
3. **Broader economic location** — identify where growth appears in BPS business participation, marketplace/non-marketplace channels, and related official statistics.
4. **Payment traces** — use Bank Indonesia measures as separate digital traces with source-native definitions, not as one-for-one sales measures.
5. **Institutional visibility** — distinguish record existence from legal reportability, identifiers, transmission, matching, verified administrative use, and observed outcomes.
6. **External corroboration** — use ASEAN/global issuer evidence only as recurrence/heterogeneity evidence, not pooled causal evidence.

The empirical package is ready for full-manuscript writing only when:

- every core issuer transition passes the admission rules;
- every number in the result register maps to a frozen source and reproducible calculation;
- BPS national, channel, and business-level evidence is frozen by publication vintage and definition;
- payment measures are separated by instrument and definition;
- institutional claims stop at the highest evidenced stage of the visibility chain;
- legacy residual/GDP/tax-gap rhetoric cannot re-enter through an old table, appendix, or README;
- all core tables and figures regenerate from frozen inputs.

The goal is research completeness, not artificial submission-readiness language.
