# Invisible Ledger V2 — Claim Boundaries

These controls apply to the revised proposal, Manuscript V2, tables, figures, appendices, presentations, and any later consolidation with Digital Tax.

## 1. Core nonclaims

The project does **not** establish that:

- gross transaction value, GMV, GTV, payment value, or platform-facilitated commerce is GDP;
- transaction value minus platform revenue is a hidden stock of money;
- the transaction-revenue difference is merchant profit, participant income, taxable income, tax liability, or a tax gap;
- platform revenue is the amount of economic activity visible to government;
- activity outside platform revenue is automatically informal, untaxed, unreported, or statistically missing;
- one accounting record ought to equal another record produced for a different institutional purpose;
- payment-system value maps one-for-one to sales, output, income, or platform transaction value;
- marketplace participation causes formalization, recordkeeping, financial-statement adoption, or lower cash use;
- the existence of a legal reporting rule proves successful transmission, taxpayer matching, enforcement, compliance gains, or revenue gains;
- the issuer sample is population-representative of Indonesian platforms;
- reconstructed Grab or Shopee country values are equivalent in evidentiary status to direct issuer/segment disclosures;
- an ASEAN or global recurrence pattern is a pooled causal estimate for Indonesia;
- the legacy Malaysia LVG exercise supplies causal validation of the revised Invisible Ledger concept;
- the old ASEAN-wide `$185B`, `$82.8B`, `12.3x`, or similar residual totals are canonical V2 findings.

## 2. Permitted claim ladder

Claims should use the strongest rung actually supported and stop there.

### A. Record existence

Permitted when a primary issuer, statistical, payment, or administrative source publishes the record.

Does not imply comparability, coverage, reportability, matching, or use.

### B. Definition and perimeter

Permitted when source-native definitions, geography, business scope, period, and accounting treatment are documented.

Does not imply that two differently defined series can be subtracted or pooled.

### C. Descriptive divergence

Permitted when two admitted records covering a defensible common perimeter imply materially different growth, level, or composition conclusions.

Does not imply one measure is wrong or that the difference is economically missing.

### D. Mechanism reconciliation

Permitted when issuer or official components can account for part of a divergence, for example incentives, gross-versus-net presentation, monetization, business mix, participation, or channel composition.

Arithmetic explanation is not automatically causal identification.

### E. Institutional reportability

Permitted when a legal or administrative source creates duties linking actor identity, transaction/turnover information, withholding, remittance, or reporting.

Does not imply completed operational linkage.

### F. Operational use

Permitted only with evidence that records are actually transmitted, matched, processed, audited, or otherwise used by the competent institution.

### G. Outcome effect

Permitted only with a design capable of identifying the claimed effect. Legal adoption, administrative totals, or before/after coincidence are not sufficient by themselves.

## 3. Language controls

Prefer:

- `different record`, `different observation layer`, `accounting boundary`, `reporting perimeter`, `commercial trace`, `administrative visibility`, `source-native measure`, `descriptive divergence`, `reconciliation`, `association`, `legal architecture`.

Avoid unless independently established:

- `hidden economy`, `unmeasured economy`, `invisible money`, `missed GDP`, `unpaid tax`, `fiscal multiplier`, `tax capture rate`, `causal validation`, `census-level estimate`, `true total`, `platforms hide`, `governments miss X%`.

## 4. Quantitative controls

Every quantitative manuscript result must carry or inherit:

- source ID / source locator;
- publication or filing vintage;
- observation period;
- geography;
- business/perimeter definition;
- metric definition;
- currency/unit;
- direct vs reconstructed status;
- transformation/calculation formula where derived;
- inclusion/exclusion rationale;
- uncertainty/sensitivity note where assumptions enter.

A value without these fields is not a frozen result.

## 5. Cross-module controls

Issuer, BPS, Bank Indonesia, and administrative records must not be pooled merely because they refer to the digital economy.

Cross-module analysis is allowed when it asks:

1. what each record measures;
2. whether the records imply the same economic interpretation;
3. why the interpretation differs;
4. what additional structure becomes visible after reconciliation.

The modules remain source-native in units and definitions unless an explicit bridge is justified.

## 6. Proposal-change rule

While the advisor-facing proposal remains under revision, the proposal outranks this pack. If the proposal changes a research question, issuer boundary, unit, or inference rule, update the research pack before drafting results from it.

No infrastructure file may silently broaden the proposal.
