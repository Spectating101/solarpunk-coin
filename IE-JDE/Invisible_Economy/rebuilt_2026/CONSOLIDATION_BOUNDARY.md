# Invisible Ledger V2 — Consolidation Boundary with Fiscal Choke Points

This file defines the interface to Digital Tax / Fiscal Choke Points while both projects are still being maximized independently.

## 1. Keep the research objects separate for now

### Invisible Ledger asks

> Where does digital-commercial growth become visible across partial economic records, when do those records imply different interpretations, and what can reconciliation recover?

Its objects are:

- transaction/activity measures;
- issuer accounting;
- business/channel statistics;
- payment traces;
- administrative visibility stages.

### Fiscal Choke Points asks

> Where and how do governments attach legally bounded fiscal responsibility inside private digital transaction infrastructure?

Its objects are:

- taxable object;
- liable node;
- destination/nexus evidence;
- transaction rail;
- reconciliation/administrative power;
- node locus and event coupling.

## 2. Permitted interface

The two projects may share the following concepts without merging their claims:

1. **non-equivalence of records** — platform revenue, transaction value, participant turnover, payment flow, GDP, and taxable base are distinct;
2. **trace-to-use distinction** — record existence is not the same as administrative usability;
3. **identity/reporting linkage** — some tax architectures transform commercial records into reportable public records;
4. **institutional visibility stages** — legal authority, transmission, matching, use, and outcomes should be separated;
5. **source and claim discipline** — every inference stops at the highest supported stage.

## 3. Forbidden premature consolidation

Until both research packages are independently frozen, do **not**:

- use an Invisible Ledger transaction-revenue difference as a Digital Tax base;
- infer tax liability from IL measurement divergence;
- use Digital Tax collection totals to validate an IL economic residual;
- rewrite IL as a tax paper;
- rewrite Fiscal Choke Points as evidence that IL's old wedge was correct;
- merge country samples simply because both projects involve platforms;
- invent a common numeric `visibility score` spanning both projects;
- claim that a fiscal choke point closes the Invisible Ledger unless transmission, matching, and verified use are actually evidenced.

## 4. Future consolidation question

After independent freezes, the joint research programme can ask:

> How does digitally mediated economic activity move from economic events, through private/statistical records, into legally and administratively usable public information, and where does that translation fail?

A possible future sequence is:

`ECONOMIC ACTIVITY`
→ `PRIVATE / COMMERCIAL TRACE`
→ `ACCOUNTING / STATISTICAL REPRESENTATION`
→ `IDENTIFIED ACTOR / JURISDICTION`
→ `LEGALLY REPORTABLE OBJECT`
→ `LIABLE / REPORTING NODE`
→ `TRANSMISSION`
→ `MATCHING / RECONCILIATION`
→ `VERIFIED ADMINISTRATIVE USE`
→ `OUTCOME`

This is a future umbrella model, not a current empirical finding.

## 5. Consolidation entry criteria

Do not start the final consolidation until:

### Invisible Ledger

- final issuer boundary is frozen;
- core numerical tables reproduce;
- BI definitions/values are frozen;
- the institutional chain is evidence-coded;
- Manuscript V2 is written from frozen results rather than legacy prose.

### Fiscal Choke Points

- country architecture and chronology are frozen;
- node/event coding survives independent re-code;
- novelty survives hostile literature review;
- legal-source currentness is refreshed;
- rival explanations are explicitly bounded.

Only then should overlap be resolved section by section.

## 6. Expected consolidation outputs later

Potential outputs, to be decided only after independent completion:

- one umbrella theory paper;
- two linked papers with a shared synthesis article;
- an integrated thesis/monograph chapter architecture;
- an evidence-governed administrative observability model usable by Policy Lab or related systems work.

No choice among these is made here.
