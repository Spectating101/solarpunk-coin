## Where to Find Prospects

### Directories
- Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA) member directory
- American Clean Power Association member list
- RE100 company list (companies committed to 100% renewable)
- PV Magazine project database
- Taiwan Solar PV Association members

### LinkedIn Search Queries
- "solar farm operator" + [region]
- "head of trading" + "renewable energy"
- "risk manager" + "solar" OR "wind"
- "VP operations" + "solar developer"
- "energy trading" + "renewable"

### Target Companies (Examples)
**Small (1-10 MW):** Local solar installers, community solar operators, rooftop aggregators
**Mid (10-50 MW):** Regional developers, C&I solar companies, independent power producers
**Large (50+ MW):** NextEra, AES, Enel, Lightsource BP, Canadian Solar, Trina Solar

### Follow-Up Cadence
- Day 0: Send initial email
- Day 3: Connect on LinkedIn (if no response)
- Day 7: Follow-up email ("Bumping this to the top of your inbox...")
- Day 14: Final follow-up with value add (attach sample risk report)
- Day 21: Move to nurture list (monthly newsletter)
