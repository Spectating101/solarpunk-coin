PROJECT OVERVIEW

SolarPunk Protocol creates a revenue floor for renewable energy producers using financial derivatives (put options). Solar/wind operators can purchase insurance that pays out when production drops below target levels due to weather variability. This transforms unpredictable revenue streams into bankable cash flows, unlocking project finance.

Problem:
Renewable energy projects face revenue volatility that blocks financing. A solar farm may produce 1000 MWh one month, then 700 MWh during monsoons or cloudy periods. Banks and lenders won't finance projects with unpredictable cash flows, creating a barrier to renewable energy deployment—especially in emerging markets.

Solution:
Smart contract-based derivatives that hedge production risk. Operators pay premiums to buy protection; option writers earn yield by providing coverage. Settlement is automated via on-chain oracles pulling solar irradiance data (NASA POWER API) and production metrics.

---

Three-layer design:

1. Settlement Layer (Solidity - EVM compatible)
