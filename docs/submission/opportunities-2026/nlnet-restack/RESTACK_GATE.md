## Why this is a real opportunity

Restack explicitly supports open-source/public-interest technology work including:
- scientific research;
- FOSS development;
- validation;
- software quality and engineering;
- security audits/formal work;
- automated testing/CI;
- documentation;
- usability/accessibility;
- packaging and deployability.

Individuals can receive grants.

## Why this is NOT yet a FIRE

Policy Lab is developed from Taiwan. Current Restack eligibility guidance says proposals from outside EU member states / Horizon Europe associated countries can be considered only when there is:

1. exceptional proposal quality;
2. unique expertise;
3. a **clear European dimension**;
4. compliance with the open-source / open-standards / open-data principles of the fund.

In addition, the new call-specific hard criteria are not yet published. Therefore a pre-September `FIRE` would be fabricated certainty.

## Native-fit thesis to test on September 3

The strongest truthful Restack framing is **not** “fund our fintech project.” It is:

> **Open evidence-boundary infrastructure for auditable automated decisions: portable schemas, deterministic policy/constraint evaluation, and verifiable decision packages that prevent downstream software from silently promoting weak source evidence into stronger authority.**

Potential open-infrastructure outputs:
- public schema/profile for evidence-to-decision objects;
- portable decision/assessment package format;
- deterministic verifier/reference implementation;
- interoperability examples with open policy/credential standards;
- reproducible packaging and independent conformance tests;
- documentation for integration without proprietary SaaS dependencies.

## European-dimension test

The proposal does **not** currently have a sufficiently concrete European dimension merely because Europe regulates digital finance or uses open standards.

A credible dimension would need at least one of:
- a European open-source/research collaborator with a concrete role;
- a European public/open-data use case that materially shapes the implementation;
- interoperability work directly useful to a European open-infrastructure community;
- adoption/evaluation by a European project or community;
- a call-specific requirement that Policy Lab’s open verification layer clearly satisfies.

Do not manufacture a Europe paragraph out of MiCA/Data Act buzzwords.
