- Energy FinTech & Smart Grid Finance.

The event is open to developers, students, researchers, professionals and teams, so Policy Lab does not face the faculty-advisor gate that blocks the current InnoServe route.

Prizes advertised:
- 1st: €7,000;
- 2nd: €4,000;
- 3rd: €2,000;
- additional partner prizes may apply.

## Why this is not currently worth pursuing

The event requires physical participation in Nicosia. From Taiwan, airfare, lodging, local transport, visa/entry logistics where applicable, and multiple days of travel create a large guaranteed cost for a probabilistic prize.

Policy Lab already exists as a mature artifact; forcing a hackathon rebuild also has high opportunity cost relative to FC, SSI, DPG and Taiwan-local academic routes.

Therefore:

> **Do not spend submission/build time on this route under current economics.**

## Reopen trigger

Reopen only if one of the following becomes concrete:
1. organizers/partner provide travel and accommodation support for shortlisted teams;
2. an external sponsor covers the Cyprus trip;
3. another high-value European event makes the marginal travel cost small;
4. remote final participation becomes officially permitted.

Until one of those changes, this is a verified opportunity but not an active target.
