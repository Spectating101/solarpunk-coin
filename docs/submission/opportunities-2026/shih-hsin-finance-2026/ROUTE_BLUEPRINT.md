## Verified scope

The conference explicitly accepts work in finance and AI, behavioral finance, capital markets, risk management, financial engineering, sustainability/ESG, and **other finance-related topics**. The headline emphasis on AI does not create an AI eligibility requirement for every paper.

No faculty-advisor requirement is stated in the official submission instructions.

## Why it is useful

This is a geographically cheap academic presentation route in Taipei. The first gate is only an abstract; the full paper is due after acceptance. That makes it useful as a local external-feedback route if the higher-value manuscript strategy changes.

## Why it is not submitted concurrently by default

The proposed Policy Lab paper would overlap materially with the FC'27 manuscript and potentially with an Asia University FTSID manuscript. Do not create duplicate simultaneous peer-reviewed submissions.

Use this route only when one of the following is true:

- the FC manuscript is not being submitted;
- the organizers confirm an abstract/presentation route that does not create conflicting publication rights and the later full paper can remain distinct/non-archival;
- a genuinely separate finance research question is developed.
