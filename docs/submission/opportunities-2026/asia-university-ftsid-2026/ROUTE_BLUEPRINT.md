## Why this route survives

The official 2026 International Conference on Financial Technology and Sustainable Innovation Development explicitly invites original work in FinTech, Sustainability & ESG, Finance & Investment Management, Digital Transformation, Blockchain & Digital Finance, and related interdisciplinary topics. It requires a full anonymized paper, and at least one author must register and present if accepted. Three Best Paper awards carry prize money and certificates; outstanding papers may be recommended to a special issue of the Asian Journal of Intelligence and Innovation, subject to separate journal review.

Policy Lab fits without inventing AI, a startup, an advisor, or a customer. It is also geographically cheap from Taiwan.

## Why this is BACKUP rather than concurrent FIRE

The primary Policy Lab proceedings manuscript is the FC'27 short paper. FC prohibits substantially overlapping concurrent peer-reviewed submissions. A FTSID paper built from the same Policy Lab mechanism, Ausgrid case, and non-promotion argument would be substantially overlapping.

Therefore:

> **Do not submit FTSID while a substantially overlapping FC manuscript is under review.**

FTSID becomes the manuscript target if:

1. FC is deliberately not submitted before September 17; or
2. FC is withdrawn/rejected early enough to submit by September 30; or
3. a genuinely distinct FTSID paper is developed with separate research questions, results, and contribution rather than repackaging FC.

## Proposed finance-facing title

> **From Evidence to Financial Authority: Auditable Constraints for Evidence-Backed Financial Claims**

## Finance-facing contribution

Unlike the security-oriented FC framing, the FTSID version should emphasize the decision architecture of evidence-backed finance:

- evidence quality is represented separately from financial admission;
- admissible claims remain quantity-bounded by explicit ceilings;
- the binding ceiling is attributable;
- settlement shortfall remains a separate economic state;
- every output is reproducible from versioned policy/evidence identities.

The outside Ausgrid case remains bounded at L0 and must not be described as an operator pilot or validated financial product.
