# Global AI Finance 2026 — Acceptance / Obligation Brief

This file exists so an acceptance cannot create a foreseeable surprise.

## Known venue facts at submission time

- event: Global AI Finance Research Conference 2026;
- dates: **2026-12-14 to 2026-12-15**;
- location: Taiwan;
- route: work-in-progress poster / extended abstract;
- submission deadline: **2026-08-31**;
- submission system: ConfTool;
- accepted posters are intended for conference presentation/program inclusion according to the public call.

## Default assumption if accepted

Assume the owner must be available **in person** for the relevant poster session in Taiwan unless the organizer later states otherwise.

Do not assume:
- remote presentation is permitted;
- absence is harmless;
- a poster can remain in the program without registration/presentation;
- registration is free.

## Known owner commitments created by submission

Submitting the abstract itself should be treated as an intention to present if accepted, subject to later registration/cost details.

The capsule must therefore make the following visible before final submission:

1. **Attendance:** likely in-person conference attendance in December.
2. **Poster:** a final physical/digital poster may be required after acceptance.
3. **Registration:** fee amount is not frozen in this repository unless/when the organizer publishes it.
4. **Travel:** because the conference is in Taiwan, travel burden is expected to be materially lower than an overseas venue but still depends on the final venue/location.
5. **Public disclosure:** title/author/abstract/poster information may appear in the conference program or website.
6. **Presentation competence:** the submitting author should be able to explain the work and answer questions; use `AUTHOR_SURPRISE_PROOFING_BRIEF.md` and `REVIEWER_QA.md`.

## Unknowns to verify immediately upon acceptance

- exact physical venue;
- exact poster-session date/time;
- poster dimensions/orientation;
- registration fee and payment deadline;
- presenter registration requirement;
- cancellation/refund policy;
- whether a poster PDF must be uploaded beforehand;
- whether the extended abstract becomes publicly downloadable or only program-listed;
- whether any copyright/license form is required;
- whether there is a camera-ready abstract revision;
- whether at least one author must attend both days or only the poster session.

## Stop conditions after acceptance

Before paying or signing anything, stop for owner approval if any of these arise:

- paid registration;
- non-refundable charge;
- copyright transfer or broad exclusive license;
- mandatory travel beyond ordinary Taiwan transit;
- mandatory publication terms not visible at original submission;
- a requirement to make a materially stronger claim than the submitted abstract;
- a conflict with FC'27 or another active publication route;
- a presenter substitution restriction affecting attendance.

## Acceptance-day protocol

When an acceptance arrives:

1. record acceptance email/date/submission ID;
2. fetch the organizer's current presenter instructions;
3. compare them against this brief;
4. flag every new cost, consent, publication, travel, or deadline requirement;
5. freeze the accepted abstract text;
6. build the final poster from `POSTER_STORYBOARD.md` without enlarging claims;
7. refresh the reviewer Q&A against any feedback from acceptance/review;
8. create one-page owner briefing with exact logistics;
9. register/pay only with owner approval where required.

## Rejection protocol

A rejection does not automatically invalidate Policy Lab. Capture any reviewer feedback, classify it as:
- comprehension failure;
- novelty/prior-art challenge;
- evidence weakness;
- venue fit;
- presentation quality;
- other.

Feed genuine criticism back into the FC/JOSS/DPG route rather than treating the outcome as a generic project-quality score.
