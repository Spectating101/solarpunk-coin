# Global AI Finance 2026 — Overlap / Ethics Guard

## Current relationship to FC'27

Policy Lab also has an active planned **Financial Cryptography 2027 short-paper** route.

The two artifacts must remain distinct in purpose:

### Global AI Finance poster

- work-in-progress poster / extended abstract;
- application-oriented demonstration of the evidence→authorization→quantity→settlement separation;
- one public outside-data checkpoint;
- intended for discussion/feedback;
- no claim of a complete formal/security contribution.

### FC'27 short paper

- anonymous peer-reviewed proceedings manuscript;
- formal/non-promotion semantics as the research contribution;
- related-work positioning against credentials, policy engines, reserve attestations, and financial-contract infrastructure;
- adversarial/integrity evaluation;
- stronger scientific novelty burden.

## Pre-submit overlap check

Before submitting the Global AI Finance poster:

- confirm the poster extended abstract is not being treated by the organizer as a peer-reviewed proceedings paper;
- confirm no copyright/publication term creates substantial-publication conflict;
- if the portal reveals proceedings/publication language not present in the public CFP, stop and reassess against the FC'27 simultaneous-submission policy.

## Content separation

The poster may state the same underlying public case facts because they are research facts, but should **not** reproduce a future FC paper's full formal model, related-work argument, adversarial evaluation, or manuscript-level novelty claims.

The FC manuscript can later cite or disclose the public poster where required by venue policy; follow FC anonymity/prior-disclosure rules at submission time.

## Authorship / AI-use integrity

The submission must be reviewable as the author's research rather than disguised generated content.

Rules:

- do not fabricate experiments, citations, related work, or reviewer-facing confidence;
- generated/editorial assistance may improve language and organization, but factual claims remain bound to repository evidence;
- the owner must be capable of explaining every sentence in the outward submission using `AUTHOR_SURPRISE_PROOFING_BRIEF.md`;
- if the venue asks for an AI-use disclosure, answer according to the exact assistance actually used and the venue's then-current wording;
- do not hide material AI assistance where explicit disclosure is required;
- do not describe Policy Lab itself as an AI system when it is not.

## Research integrity

- public data does not equal source-holder validation;
- controlled fixtures do not equal empirical field evidence;
- scenario stress does not equal observed settlement;
- deterministic reproduction does not equal external truth;
- policy choice does not equal economic optimality;
- acceptance does not equal project validation beyond the scope of what the venue accepted.

## Withdrawal triggers

Withdraw or correct the poster if, before presentation:

- a material numerical result is found to be wrong;
- evidence identity/provenance changes invalidate the submitted result;
- the public case can no longer be reproduced;
- the outward abstract is discovered to contain a materially false stronger inference;
- publication terms create a conflict with the primary manuscript route that cannot be resolved.

Minor wording improvements should not be used to rewrite the historical submitted artifact; preserve the submission version and prepare an explicitly revised presentation version if the venue permits it.
