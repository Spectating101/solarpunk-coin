# Policy Lab — Route-Specific Submission Adapters

**Purpose:** adapt one truthful project to different evaluation environments without changing the project to chase a score.

Source basis: frozen Gauntlet Simulator v1.1 + current Policy Lab machine evidence.

## Core rule

Never use packaging to repair a failed semantic or maturity gate.

Packaging can improve clarity. It cannot create:

- AI contribution;
- owner/operator validation;
- institutional pilot evidence;
- commercialization carrier;
- sociotechnical contribution;
- legal authority;
- market adoption.

## InnoServe ADIAI 2026

### Current posture

**FIRE — packaging is high leverage.**

The frozen Gauntlet model rates the route `COMPETITIVE` and identifies packaging/demo clarity as unusually valuable relative to another core subsystem.

### Lead pitch

> **Policy Lab is a practical accountability layer for evidence-backed financial decisions: it shows what external evidence can actually authorize, how much it can justify, and exactly why a stricter rule can block the same evidence.**

### 15-second problem

> “Automated finance increasingly depends on external data, but the rule that converts data into financial authority is often hidden. That makes it easy to confuse available data with trustworthy evidence, admission with unlimited quantity, or a valid claim with guaranteed settlement.”

### Demo emphasis

1. outside Ausgrid checkpoint;
2. L0 preserved;
3. 33.066 under open policy;
4. same evidence blocked under pilot policy;
5. 40% settlement shortfall;
6. receipt/reproduction.

### Map to likely final-stage criteria

**Innovation:** explicit evidence → authority → quantity → settlement separation; deterministic binding attribution.

**Practicality:** shows a concrete way to audit evidence-backed automated decisions; architecture can apply where external facts authorize financial consequences.

**Technical quality:** deterministic core, typed schemas, versioned policies, outside-data CI, portable assessment, replay.

**Presentation:** visually teach “same evidence / different policy / different consequence” before explaining internal ontology.

### Avoid

- leading with R1–R4;
- spending presentation time on historical SPK;
- pretending there is an operator pilot;
- manufacturing a hardware story because hardware demos are competitive.

## III AI Innovation Challenge 2026

### Current posture

**CONDITIONAL — do not submit unless genuine AI contribution already exists and is score-bearing.**

### Gate question

Can we point to a real AI method that materially affects the evaluated system and withstand judge scrutiny?

If no: **do not submit Policy Lab merely by attaching an LLM.**

If yes, the pitch must clearly separate:

```text
AI contribution
from
Policy Lab deterministic authority
```

The AI must not become an unreviewable decision authority that undermines the project's main contribution.

### If gate passes

Lead with the industry problem and show how AI supports evidence interpretation or analysis while deterministic policy remains the final bounded authority.

### Avoid

- “AI-powered Policy Lab” with no substantive method;
- chat interface as innovation;
- allowing model output to silently promote evidence status.

## FinTech Taipei Awards 2026

### Current posture

**LOW-COST SUBMISSION ONLY unless external validation materially improves.**

The current weakness is structural, not mainly presentation: mixed fintech competition includes startups, vendors, institutions and deployed products where pilots are normal evidence.

### Best truthful pitch

> **Policy Lab is auditable policy infrastructure for evidence-backed finance: it makes the rule between external evidence and financial authority explicit, quantity-bounded and reproducible.**

### Finance problem framing

Potential financial failures:

- asset or certificate claims outrun source evidence;
- policy changes are hard to audit after the fact;
- external data is accepted without explicit provenance threshold;
- collateral/claim quantities are not tied to inspectable ceilings;
- settlement failure is hidden behind successful issuance.

### What to show

- deterministic policy decision;
- binding constraint attribution;
- portable assessment/receipt;
- settlement failure;
- explicit governance/non-claim boundary.

### State the weakness cleanly

> “The current public checkpoint is outside public data, not a financial-institution pilot. The next validation gate is one attributable owner/operator source or institutional evaluation.”

### Stop rule

Do not spend multiple development days adding commercial-looking features to repair the absence of a pilot.

## NSTC Research Entrepreneurship

### Current posture

**HOLD.**

The route expects more than an impressive artifact. Current missing pieces include:

- eligible institutional commercialization route;
- commercialization carrier/team;
- market validation;
- credible milestones and budget;
- pathway from research asset to deployment.

### Reconsider only when

At least one real commercialization carrier or pilot path exists.

Packaging alone should not flip this route to FIRE.

## Financial Cryptography 2027

### Current posture

**PRESERVE AS A SERIOUS RESEARCH ROUTE.**

The key gate is publishable research novelty and rigorous evaluation, not conventional startup traction.

### Research pitch

> **Policy Lab is an executable methodology for bounding evidence-backed financial claims. It separates evidence sufficiency, policy authority, quantity constraints and settlement, then preserves the exact stopping point as a deterministic research object.**

### Candidate contribution structure

1. formal object model for evidence-backed claims;
2. explicit admission and quantity semantics;
3. deterministic binding attribution;
4. settlement as separate failure layer;
5. non-promotion across evidence/research boundaries;
6. controlled cases + outside-data checkpoint;
7. reproducibility and portable assessment package.

### Research questions to sharpen before paper submission

- What class of errors becomes observable only after separating admission, quantity and settlement?
- Which properties of the deterministic identity model are novel relative to policy engines / financial contracts / oracle systems?
- Can the framework be formalized as safety properties or invariants?
- What negative or adversarial cases demonstrate necessity rather than merely implementation completeness?
- What does the outside-data case establish beyond a worked example?

### Avoid

- marketing language about “revolutionizing finance”;
- treating software size as scientific novelty;
- relying on interface polish as evidence.

## ACM FAccT 2027

### Current posture

**CONDITIONAL — possible only if a genuine sociotechnical accountability argument exists.**

Software auditability by itself is not enough.

### Potential legitimate angle

The strongest possible route is about **where institutional authority hides in automated evidence-backed financial systems**:

- who chooses provenance thresholds;
- who defines admissibility;
- who benefits from quantity ceilings;
- what happens when uncertainty is encoded as policy;
- which stakeholders can challenge the decision;
- what institutional assumptions are made visible or remain hidden.

### Requirement

A FAccT submission would need empirical/institutional or governance analysis beyond the current deterministic engine.

Do not rebrand a software paper as sociotechnical research.

## Reusable application formula

For any new opportunity:

```text
1. verify eligibility
2. verify semantic fit
3. identify actual rubric
4. identify maturity expectation
5. choose one Policy Lab value proposition that is genuinely native
6. map existing proof to score-bearing criteria
7. state structural gaps
8. estimate marginal packaging value
9. FIRE / CHEAP / HOLD / KILL
```

Never start by rewriting Policy Lab to resemble last year's winner.
