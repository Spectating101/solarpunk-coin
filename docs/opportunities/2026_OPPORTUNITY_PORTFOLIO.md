```text

→ open-source builder infrastructure only when a live Wishlist / RFP item genuinely matches
```

## Conflict and rule matrix

### FITI

- Existing technology is expected and commercialization is central.
- Team participation is required.
- Major program activities are mandatory for the technology owner.
- Business-plan and buyer evidence matter.

### ETHGlobal

- Classic track requires from-scratch work after official kickoff.
- Pre-existing project-specific code, designs, and assets are prohibited in Classic.
- Continuity tracks may allow disclosed existing code if that track is actually available and its rules are followed.
- Do not prebuild a hackathon project and later call it from-scratch.

### Presidential Hackathon

- Public/private co-creation and government open data are central.
- The Energy Standard product narrative must be translated into a public problem and public-data workflow.
- Exact 2026 submission timing/rules must be verified directly in the registration system before investment.

### NASA Space Apps

- Challenges are released closer to the hackathon.
- Teams are not allowed to begin work on challenge solutions before the hackathon.
- Challenge-specific code/design work therefore waits.

### ESP

- Grant-funded work must improve Ethereum and be free/open-source/publicly accessible.
- Current applications must match a live Wishlist or RFP item.
- The whole SolarPunk workbench is not automatically an ESP grant fit.

### JOSS

- Requires meaningful research-software significance and feature-complete, maintainable software.
- Requires at least six months of public development history before submission.
- Generative-AI use must be disclosed comprehensively.
