## 1. Why this is the highest-priority non-dilutive opportunity

FITI is explicitly designed as the first mile from academic research to entrepreneurship and provides six months of business training, staged evaluation, mentoring, commercialization resources, and staged financial incentives.

The 2026-2 rules require:

- at least 50% of the team to be current students, graduates within one year, or full-time researchers at eligible academic/research institutions;
- an original technology/concept in innovative technology or health/medical technology with commercialization potential;
- a team application;
- a recommended team size of 2–5;
- recommendation from a full-time assistant professor or above at a team member's academic institution;
- a business plan, presentation, team/personal information, signed forms, and related application materials.

The official FAQ states that currently enrolled foreign students and overseas Chinese students are treated the same as domestic students for eligibility.

The 2026-2 funding structure included:

```text
public-call initial selection     NT$10,000
pass evaluation stage 1           +NT$50,000
pass evaluation stage 2           +NT$200,000
Outstanding Entrepreneurship Award NT$1,000,000 startup fund
```

The NT$1 million startup fund requires qualifying company registration and is paid to the startup company's bank account under the program's timing rules.

## 6. FITI business plan mapped to the official suggested structure

The official 2026-2 template suggests seven major areas.

### 6.1 技術特性和產品優勢 — Technology and product advantage

Application content:

```text
problem: mixed evidence and policy reasoning is difficult to reproduce
technical method: typed evidence/context/policy/decision objects
admission gates: block before quantity evaluation
quantity ceilings: comparable common-unit limits
binding attribution: identify the rule determining the maximum
settlement stage: separate settlement from admission
receipt: deterministic decision identity and inspection artifact
```

Benchmark against categories, not strawmen:

```text
spreadsheet/manual policy workflow
rules engines / decision platforms
research notebooks
green-attribute registries
energy modeling tools
```

Competitive claim:

> The differentiation is not generic policy-as-code or solar modeling. It is an evidence-aware case method that explicitly separates observed/controlled evidence, modeled context, declared policy, derived bounded quantity, and settlement outcome while retaining decision identity and binding-rule attribution.

### 6.5 關鍵痛點 — Critical pain / NABC

FITI guidance explicitly suggests Need → Approach → Benefit → Competition.

Draft NABC:

**Need**

Energy-linked and sustainability claim decisions can depend on heterogeneous data and internal rules that are difficult to reconstruct later.

**Approach**

Normalize evidence, classify assurance, evaluate explicit policy rules, attribute the blocking/binding rule, and emit a deterministic receipt.

**Benefit**

Reduce manual reconstruction, make evidence limitations visible, and create a repeatable audit/research artifact.

**Competition**

The product does not replace energy modeling tools or generic decision engines; it binds evidence semantics to explicit bounded-decision and receipt workflows.
