PROJECT DESCRIPTION (500 words max)

SolarPunk Protocol solves a critical barrier to renewable energy deployment: revenue volatility blocks financing.

The Problem:
Solar and wind farms face unpredictable production due to weather. A solar installation may generate 1000 MWh one month, then 700 MWh during monsoons. Banks won't lend against these unstable cash flows, preventing billions in renewable energy projects—especially in emerging markets with highest solar potential.

Our Solution:
Financial derivatives (put options) that create a revenue floor for renewable producers. Operators purchase insurance that pays out when production drops below target. This transforms volatile revenue into bankable cash flow, unlocking project finance.

Technical Architecture:
1. Smart Contracts (Solidity): Hold collateral, enforce automated settlements, manage liquidations
2. Pricing Engine (Python): Calculate premiums using Black-Scholes framework validated on 3 years NASA solar data
3. Oracle Layer: Chainlink integration for solar irradiance data and production metrics
4. User Interface (React): Dashboard for operators to hedge, monitor, and settle positions

WHY ESP SUPPORT IS CRITICAL

1. Security Audit: The protocol cannot move from testnet pilot to L2 mainnet without professional review. Custody-bearing contracts handling option settlement and stability pool reserves require the kind of audit only EF-tier funding makes accessible to a solo academic researcher.

2. Oracle Productionisation: Today's NASA → Sepolia keeper runs on a centralised GitHub Actions cron. Migrating to Chainlink Automation + Functions removes that single point of failure and turns the oracle layer into something that genuinely belongs on Ethereum.

3. L2 Ecosystem Integration: Sepolia is a proving ground; Arbitrum/Optimism is where energy operators in emerging markets can actually use this. ESP support connects this work to the L2 ecosystem and Chainlink BD relationships needed to ship.

4. Climate Public Good: Energy-revenue derivatives are the missing primitive for renewable project finance. Banks won't lend against weather-dependent cash flows; the protocol turns volatile production into bankable cash flow. The code is MIT, the methods are reproducible, and the data is open NASA POWER.

5. Academic Rigor: The work is being done as a Master's thesis with a published Python pricing library, formal stress-test memo, and intended journal submissions. ESP support takes that research from prototype to peer-reviewable infrastructure.
