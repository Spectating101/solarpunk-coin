# WHY NOW

## The short answer

Because SolarPunk is already beyond concept stage, but still before external proof and audit. That is exactly when targeted funding has high leverage.

## Why timing matters

- The protocol mechanism is implemented and testable now.
- The remaining blockers are externalization and security credibility.
- Without funding, progress slows on deploy publication, security packaging, and pilot prep.

## What would happen without funding

- slower path to public testnet proof
- weaker audit-readiness velocity
- delayed pilot-facing execution

## What funding changes immediately

1. converts local proof into public proof
2. converts technical quality into reviewer confidence
3. converts architecture into pilot-facing integration materials

## Tone for external use

Do not claim dominance or mass adoption.  
Do claim clear mechanism quality, clear next milestones, and clear funding-to-output mapping.
