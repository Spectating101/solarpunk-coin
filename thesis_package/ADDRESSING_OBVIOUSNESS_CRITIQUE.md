## 📊 WHAT'S NOT OBVIOUS: THE STRUCTURAL BREAK

### If Energy Anchoring Were "Obvious":

Then β should be CONSTANT over time:
```
2020-2021 (concentrated):   β = -0.20
2022-2023 (decentralized):  β = -0.20  ← "obvious" prediction
```

### What You ACTUALLY Found:

```
2020-2021 (concentrated):   β = -0.206***
2021-2022 (decentralized):  β = -0.080  (61% weaker!)

Chow test: F = 4.786, p = 0.0009
```

**This is NOT obvious!** It could have gone three ways:
1. β stays same (energy always matters equally)
2. β gets stronger (decentralization increases cost sensitivity)
3. β gets weaker (your finding)

You TESTED a non-obvious hypothesis and found #3.

## 💭 PHILOSOPHICAL POINT: "OBVIOUS" IN HINDSIGHT

**Many great findings seem obvious AFTER you know the answer:**

- "Smoking causes cancer" → "Duh, you're inhaling toxins"
- "Hand-washing prevents disease" → "Obviously bacteria spread"
- "Stock returns are unpredictable" → "Markets are efficient, duh"

**But BEFORE the research:**
- Doctors smoked in hospitals
- Surgeons didn't wash hands
- Technical analysis was mainstream

**Your finding will seem obvious to some because:**
1. You explained it clearly (good!)
2. They're reasoning backward from your result (common bias)
3. They don't realize the null hypothesis was plausible

**Don't let "obvious in hindsight" discourage you.**
