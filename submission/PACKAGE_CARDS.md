## Card 2A — IEEE ICDLT 2026

**Package ID:** `P2-ICDLT-2026-001`  
**Role:** preferred technical systems paper  
**Deadline:** 2026-08-31  
**Effort class:** high  
**Return:** technical peer review, IEEE-branded venue, possible durable proceedings pathway

### Working title

> **Evidence-Gated Digital Claims: A Deterministic Constraint and Receipt Architecture for Distributed Ledgers**

### Central question

> How can external evidence, versioned policy, bounded quantity, settlement, and lineage be evaluated deterministically for digital claims?

### Contribution stack

1. explicit separation of admission, quantity, identity, and settlement;
2. deterministic decision and receipt semantics;
3. binding-limit attribution;
4. counterfactual replay with evidence/context identity preserved;
5. controlled evaluation and failure cases.

### Required deliverables

- double-anonymous paper within venue limits;
- related-work comparison;
- reproducible evaluation matrix;
- exported decision/settlement receipts;
- technical architecture figure;
- artifact availability statement;
- overlap check against FTSID.

### New work required

- evaluation suite;
- baseline or comparison framework;
- edge/adversarial cases;
- concise technical specification;
- venue formatting.

### Go rule

Proceed only when the technical novelty and evaluation are strong enough to carry the paper without relying on the broader monetary narrative.

### Stop rule

Switch to BCK if the paper remains architecture-led and evaluation-light by the route decision gate.
