## 4. Weekly operating cadence

### Monday — gate review

- update only evidence-bearing status changes;
- identify the single programme bottleneck;
- reject work that does not advance an active gate;
- confirm privacy, permission, and non-claim boundaries.

### Midweek — production

- execute source intake, research audit, benchmark, or package production;
- keep venue-local language subordinate to canonical registers;
- record defects and scope changes caused by external evidence.

### Friday — proof review

- inspect new artifacts, hashes, tests, review notes, or external records;
- update the scoreboard;
- close completed work or explicitly record blockers;
- publish no promotional claim lacking corresponding evidence.

## 5. Status rules

Use only these programme statuses:

- `NOT STARTED`
- `HYPOTHESIS ONLY`
- `SCOPED`
- `ACTIVE`
- `BLOCKED ON EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY`
- `PARTIAL`
- `INTERNAL COMPLETE`
- `EXTERNALLY REVIEWED`
- `ACCEPTED`
- `REJECTED`
- `ARCHIVED`

`INTERNAL COMPLETE` must never be presented as external validation. `EXTERNALLY REVIEWED` must identify the reviewer, scope, date, and resulting corrections. `ACCEPTED` must identify the authority or programme making the decision.

---

## 6. Value review

Every proposed tranche must answer:

1. Which maturity gate does this advance?
2. What new external, reproducible, institutional, commercial, or intellectual evidence will exist afterward?
3. What claim becomes permissible that is not permissible today?
4. What work will be stopped if the expected evidence does not appear?
5. Does the tranche preserve founder ownership, privacy, research accuracy, and public/private boundaries?

Work that cannot answer these questions remains deferred.
