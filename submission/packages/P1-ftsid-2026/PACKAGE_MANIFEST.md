## 1. Conversion function

Convert the whole CL–ECI programme into one bounded finance and institutional paper. The durable residual output is a complete integrated manuscript even if the selected venue rejects it or timing requires a successor venue.

## 2. Central question

> Under what empirical, evidentiary, and institutional conditions can energy-related information support a bounded digital financial claim?

## 3. Central proposition

> Physical grounding or statistical informativeness does not itself create financial discipline. A defensible claim requires purpose-appropriate evidence, explicit authority, bounded quantity, separately represented settlement, controlled identity and reuse, and reproducible governance of the complete decision path.

## 4. Unique contribution

1. signal, evidence, authority, quantity, settlement, and money as separate analytical stages;
2. ECI as purpose-indexed empirical admissibility;
3. Constrained Ledger as institutional closure;
4. Norway as bounded comparative institutional evidence;
5. Policy Lab as an executable research method for blocking, binding attribution, replay, settlement stress, and reproducible receipts;
6. a formal path from controlled evaluation toward external evidence and conformance without claiming those gates before completion.

- completed energy-backed currency;
- Norwegian deployment, endorsement, or validation;
- legal issuance or settlement authority;
- external validation from a scaffold or controlled case;
- benchmark PASS before an archived successful run;
- neutral-standard status from the founding benchmark;
- demonstrated circulation, liquidity, stable value, acceptance, or unit-of-account use;
- production security or deployment readiness;
- customer, market, or adoption evidence not contained in the paper’s frozen scope.

## 11. Overlap requirements

P1 may share P0 doctrine and controlled artifacts with P2, but its central question, contribution hierarchy, manuscript, discussion, and conclusion remain finance/institutional. P2 must contribute a distinct technical evaluation rather than restating P1 with implementation detail.

## 12. Go / stop rules

### Go when

- the bounded central proposition survives claim audit;
- each section uses only frozen claim IDs;
- central empirical statements have explicit result-ledger status;
- Norway claims map to frozen sources;
- controlled evidence is labeled honestly and reproduced;
- benchmark and external-case status are exact;
- the paper remains valuable without deployment or monetary claims.

### Remove or reduce a component when

- an empirical result does not reproduce;
- an institutional source does not support the proposed wording;
- a controlled receipt cannot be regenerated;
- benchmark validation is incomplete;
- external-case permission or review is incomplete.

### Stop or withdraw the paper when

- unreproduced empirical claims carry the central contribution;
- Norway is presented as validation;
- the manuscript becomes mostly software documentation;
- P2 is materially the same manuscript;
- venue timing would require weakening the claim and evidence controls.
