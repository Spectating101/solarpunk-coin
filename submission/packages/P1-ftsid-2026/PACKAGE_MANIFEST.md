## 1. Conversion function

Convert the whole CL–ECI programme into one bounded finance/institutional paper. The durable residual output is a complete integrated manuscript even if the venue rejects it.

## 2. Central question

> Under what empirical and institutional conditions can energy evidence credibly constrain a digital financial claim?

## 3. Central proposition

> Physical reality or statistical informativeness does not itself create financial discipline. Credible constraint requires purpose-appropriate evidence, authorized action, bounded quantity, explicit uncertainty, unique claim identity, defined settlement, anti-reuse, and limited governance.

## 4. Unique contribution

1. conceptual boundary chain from signal to money;
2. ECI as purpose-indexed empirical admissibility;
3. CL as institutional closure;
4. Norway as bounded comparative institutional evidence;
5. Policy Lab as an executable method for blocking, binding attribution, replay, and settlement stress.
