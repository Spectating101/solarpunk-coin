## 1. Frozen paper identity

### Working title

> **From Energy Signals to Evidence-Governed Financial Claims: An Executable Institutional Framework**

The historical title “From Energy Signals to Monetary Constraints” may remain as a programme subtitle, but the manuscript must not imply that a completed monetary system has been demonstrated.

### Central question

> Under what empirical, evidentiary, and institutional conditions can energy-related information support a bounded digital financial claim?

### Central answer

> Physical grounding or statistical informativeness is insufficient by itself. A defensible claim requires purpose-appropriate evidence, explicit authority, bounded quantity, separately represented settlement, controlled identity and reuse, and reproducible governance of the complete decision path.

### Contribution hierarchy

1. **Boundary doctrine:** signal, evidence, authority, quantity, settlement, and money are different analytical stages.
2. **Empirical admissibility:** ECI evaluates whether information deserves operational weight for a specified purpose.
3. **Institutional closure:** Constrained Ledger specifies the controls required after evidence becomes relevant.
4. **Executable method:** Policy Lab turns those distinctions into versioned cases, policies, decisions, receipts, and replay.
5. **Comparative grounding:** Norway illustrates analogous separation in mature energy institutions without validating the proposed system.
6. **Evaluation:** controlled cases demonstrate deterministic behavior; external cases may extend, but are not required to fabricate validity.
