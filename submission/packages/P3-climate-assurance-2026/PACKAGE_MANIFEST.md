## 1. Conversion function

Convert the programme’s institutional and technical architecture into one usable and inspectable workflow for external energy or sustainability evidence.

This package is not the programme’s final market definition. It is the first executable proving domain for evidence-governed claims.

## 2. User and research problem

External claims can fail through:

- ambiguous source relationship or custody;
- unresolved timestamps, units, intervals, or sign conventions;
- unsupported assurance promotion;
- insufficient admission authority;
- excessive or unbounded quantity;
- duplicate use or missing cancellation;
- untracked correction;
- settlement that does not close against the admitted decision;
- inability to reproduce the result from the declared artifacts.

## 3. Product proposition

> Policy Lab shows which evidence was supplied, how it was interpreted, which rule admitted or blocked the case, which quantity ceiling bound it, what changed after correction or counterfactual policy selection, and which exact artifacts reproduce the decision.

The product does not issue a certificate, certify a physical meter, replace a regulator or accredited verifier, or create money.

## 4. Required workflow

```text
source artifact
→ custody and permission
→ semantic and temporal mapping
→ diagnostics
→ actual evidence and assurance state
→ purpose-specific policy evaluation
→ blocked or bounded decision
→ optional declared settlement
→ reproducible receipt and capsule
→ source-holder and external review
```

Identity, transfer, cancellation, anti-reuse, and correction are later lifecycle modules. They may be exercised through controlled benchmarks, but product expansion must not outrun external evidence.

## 8. Route derivatives

### Research and technical demonstrations

Emphasize architecture, benchmark, external-case evidence, reproducibility, and limitations.

### Sustainability and fintech competitions

Use one simplified product identity. Do not create a separate ontology or claim market validation.

### Long-duration sustainability challenge

Use the programme to obtain Cases 002–003, stakeholder feedback, impact evidence, and external review.

### Blockchain for Good or ecosystem funding

Apply only when shared identity, transfer, cancellation, anti-reuse, or correction across parties creates a specific need for blockchain infrastructure.

## 10. Forbidden claims

- legal or official certificate issuance, transfer, or cancellation;
- physical meter or device certification;
- prevention of fraud or double counting outside the modeled system boundary;
- real registry integration without attributable evidence;
- production security or operational readiness;
- customer adoption, willingness to pay, or demand without evidence;
- financial backing, stable value, circulation, redemption, or monetary status;
- industry-standard or neutral-protocol status before independent adoption.

## 11. Go / stop rules

### Go when

- work advances External Cases 001–003, conformance, independent review, publication, or attributable institutional use;
- the reviewer can reproduce the flagship path;
- evidence, policy, quantity, and settlement identities remain separate;
- public and private data boundaries remain explicit.

### Stop or reduce scope when

- work expands into a token, marketplace, generalized enterprise platform, or full registry before external evidence;
- the demonstration depends on unverifiable live integration;
- documentation and tests fall behind feature growth;
- product claims outrun source, review, use, or payment evidence;
- a deadline would require weakening the programme’s claim and evidence controls.
